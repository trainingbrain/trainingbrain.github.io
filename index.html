Why Standardized Cognitive Benchmarks Matter

Unlike generic mobile brain games that only train you to become better at the game itself, standardized neuropsychological instruments are designed to isolate specific neural circuits:

  • Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex (DLPFC - Brodmann Area 9/46): Engaged during the Wisconsin Card Sorting Test and N-Back memory updating tasks.
  • Anterior Cingulate Cortex (ACC): Responsible for conflict monitoring and suppressing prepotent responses during the Stroop interference task.
  • Fronto-Parietal Network: Coordinates visual spatial scanning and motor-visual coordination in the Trail Making Test.
